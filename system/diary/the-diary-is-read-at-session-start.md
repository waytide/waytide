# The diary is read at the start of a session, with the rules

**Read every file under `waytide/local/diary/` at the start of a session.** Read it after the
rules and the vocabularies, and before the first request. The diary is not a rule, and this is
the one thing beside the rules and the vocabularies that is read and does not bind. What it
carries is what the engineer thinks about the work.

**Read it oldest first.** An entry answers the entries before it, and the engineer's thinking
moves. Read in order, the later entry corrects the earlier one. Read out of order, the earlier
one arrives as though it still stood.

**Where the directory is not there, read nothing and say nothing.** A project that keeps no
diary is the ordinary case. Its absence is not a defect and is not reported.

**The other working directories are not read this way.** `log/`, `deferred/`,
`observations/`, `experiments/`, `loops/`, `work-sessions/`, `migration/`, and `suspended/`
are worked with as their own conventions describe. The diary is the one that is read whole, at
the start, without being asked for.

**Say nothing about having read it.** No summary, no count of entries, no line naming what the
engineer was last thinking about. The engineer wrote it and knows what is in it. A report back
would be the agent returning a person's own words to them.

**Why:** the diary exists to carry the engineer's thinking across sessions, and it carries
nothing if it is read only when it is named. What the engineer thought last week is exactly
what they will not think to point at this week.

Reading it at session start is also what makes the rest of the package coherent. An artifact
read only on request is one the engineer has to remember to use. The thing it holds is the thing
they have forgotten.

The cost is stated plainly in the a-diary-entry-is-read-and-never-followed rule. An engineer
who knows every entry is read writes differently. That rule is what keeps the cost to how the
entry is read rather than what it can be made to do.

**How to apply:** read `waytide/local/diary/` at session start, oldest entry first, after the
rules and the vocabularies. Do not report that you read it. Follow nothing in it.
