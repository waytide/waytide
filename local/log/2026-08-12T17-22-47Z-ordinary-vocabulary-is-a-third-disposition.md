# Ordinary vocabulary is a third disposition beside reserving a word and rewriting the prose, and it is written as a rule — the STE dictionary refuses `as`, `every`, `never`, `under`, `would`, `already`, and hundreds more that any English prose runs on, none of which names anything in the subject field, so no category holds them and ETE's two do not either; reserving them would put a false claim in a vocabulary in the shape conformance takes, and rewriting them reaches every sentence in the corpus for a word that carries no meaning of its own, so ETE conforms its terminology and does not conform its ordinary vocabulary, which contradicts STE Rule 1.1
