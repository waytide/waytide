# The Waytide vocabularies record about 2% of the words the STE dictionary does not approve, so the corpus's technical vocabulary is used rather than recorded — the corpus's prose holds 3,651 distinct word forms, of which 383 are approved, 433 are not approved with a stated alternative, and 2,880 are absent; the seven vocabularies declare 78 terms, covering 25 of the not-approved and 52 of the absent; what is declared is the coined vocabulary such as `actuation` and `hinge`, while the ordinary technical nouns the work runs on — `test`, `file`, `design`, `implementation`, `branch`, `state` — are undeclared, so STE Rule 1.6's permission for a not-approved word used as a technical noun is not being claimed for them
