# A lens names a set of positions on one matter, and the system is full of them

- **State:** Lapsed
- **Tags:** [observation]

**Why this state:** **set aside 2026-08-18 as a likely dead end**, on the engineer's judgment, the same day it was written. It is kept as the discovery record rather than deleted, which is what an observation is for. Nothing in `waytide/system/` was ever changed for it.

**What was found is still true and is why the record is kept.** Ten sets of positions exist in the
system and none is named as an instance of a kind. That survey stands whether or not `lens` is the
word for it.

**Two things weigh against the idea, and the second was found after it was written.** The term is a
figure the name-literally-not-by-analogy rule would have to be argued past. And **the word is
already in the corpus in a nearby sense** — `lib-report-format` and `test-report-format` each
classify "along the lenses below", meaning ways of looking at a body of material for a report. A
term that has to displace its own near-synonym inside the same system is starting from behind.

**What would revive it** is a rule that needs to say *this is one of those* and has no word for it.
No rule has needed that.

## The term

**A lens is a set of positions on one matter.** It is a perspective the matter is looked at
through, and it discriminates one thing from another along a single axis. **Direction and
sequencing was a lens**, and it was dropped as a pairing on 2026-08-18 while its underlying
relationship was kept — see the one-ideas-directory aspiration.

**The positions can be entirely ad hoc.** Nothing constrains them to two, to a settled vocabulary,
or to a shape shared with any other lens. What makes a lens a lens is that its positions answer
one question about the same subject.

## The system is full of them, and none is named as one

Each of these is a set of positions on one matter, stated in some rule and recognized nowhere as
an instance of a kind:

- **attended / unattended** — the human's level of participation. `design-by-efferent`.
- **subtle / load-bearing** — the two marks of a hinge, which is a lens whose positions are
  required together rather than chosen between.
- **deferred / suspended / out of scope** — *not yet*, *no longer*, *not here*. `foundation`'s
  vocabulary states these three beside each other precisely because nothing else did, which is a
  lens being drawn by hand.
- **experiment / feature** — a question against an intent. The whole of why the two lifecycles
  differ.
- **Active / Realized / Deferred / Superseded / Abandoned / Suspended** — what became of an idea.
- **Affirmed / Refuted / Inconclusive / Abandoned / Superseded / Suspended** — the verdict on an
  experiment's question.
- **formal / intuitive / OODA** — the mode.
- **rule / observation** — binding against not yet binding.
- **historical record / live artifact** — left as written against reconciled. This one is the
  least visible and the most consequential, since it decides whether an artifact may be edited at
  all.
- **merged / unmerged** — which decides what a concluding log entry carries.

**The recurring cost of not naming the kind** is that each lens is argued from scratch where it is
introduced. `foundation`'s vocabulary spends a section stating the three ways of saying *not now*
beside each other, and says outright that they "are confused most where nothing distinguishes
them". That is the work a named kind would carry once.

## A lens describes the schema. It is never written into it

**Corrected 2026-08-18.** This record first proposed a `**Lens:**` line on a record. That was
wrong twice over: it put the axis name in every record when **the field name is the axis**, and it
treated a lens as something a record carries rather than as a way of describing what a field
already does.

**`lens` is a metaphor for the schema, and it stays outside it.** Nothing writes the word into an
artifact. It is the word a writer uses when saying what a canonical line *is*.

**Seen that way, four of the system's ten canonical lines are lenses**, and the difference is
checkable. A lens field's value comes from a **set of positions**. Every other field carries a
value.

| Line | Lens? | The matter it is a perspective on |
|---|---|---|
| `**State:**` (41 uses) | yes | what became of the record |
| `**Kind:**` (3) | yes | what the suspended thing was |
| `**State:**` (2) | yes | how settled an observation's thinking is |
| `**Priority:**` (8) | yes, with ad hoc positions | rank in the queue |
| `**Tags:**` (12) | no | freeform, no position set |
| `**Summary:**`, `**Waits for:**`, `**Origin:**`, `**Suspended:**`, `**Reconciliations:**` | no | each carries a value rather than a position |

**So the schema addition the lenses suggest is not a lens field.** It is that recognizing a lens
tells a writer a field is available where the positions are currently argued in prose. The ten
lenses this record lists are mostly in the second condition: `attended / unattended` and
`experiment / feature` are stated in rules and written into no line.

**Whether any of them wants a line is unsettled**, and no artifact has asked for one. The idea's
**kind**, which the one-ideas-directory aspiration leaves open, is the nearest candidate — it is a
lens with six positions and no field.

## What this is not

**It is not a proposal to restate every lens in a common form.** The lenses above are stated where
they are stated, argued in their own words, and each is correct as written. Naming the kind gives a
writer a word for what they are doing. It obliges nothing of what already exists.

**It is not a vocabulary entry yet.** `lens` is a figure — an optical instrument — and it is
**held to be metaphorical on purpose**, describing the schema rather than naming a part of it. The
name-literally-not-by-analogy rule rejects a metaphor a reader must be taught, and keeps `hinge`
and `cradle` on the grounds that they are precise, in active use, and defined plainly. A word whose
whole job is to describe rather than to be written is a case that rule has not met. Whether `lens`
earns the standing `hinge` has is a question for the reservation, not for this record.
