# A log entry for an idea names the record and what happened to it, and never asserts the idea's content as fact

- **State:** Realized
- **Tags:** [aspiration]

## Premise

**As the engineer stated it:** the logs that pertain to an idea shouldn't be written as if the
idea has been implemented, which is what they presently do.

**An idea settles a direction. It builds nothing.** A log entry that states the idea's content in
the indicative mood says the system behaves that way. Where the idea is `Open` or `Active`, the
system does not.

## The corpus carries the two forms

**The recent entries assert the content.** Six were written on 2026-08-24 for the
a-package-checks-whether-it-is-current-when-it-loads idea, which is `Open`:

- *The package currency check belongs to the rule set read at the engineer's first prompt, not to
  the session-start hook*
- *The currency check's first prompt asks whether to check for updates at all*
- *An unclean working tree or an unreachable remote is reported and the update is not offered*

**None of that exists.** No package checks its currency, no prompt is put, and no record of a
declined check is written. A reader of the log meets three statements about a system that does not
do any of it.

**The commit subjects carried the same claim**, which is the more-scanned surface — *The first
prompt asks whether to check*, *The composite runs the currency check too*, *An unclean tree is
reported, not offered*.

**The earlier entries name the record.** Each states what was recorded, what kind of record it is,
and why it carries the state it carries:

- *Waytide for content and web is recorded as an idea — … Open because whether content conventions
  and Hugo conventions are one package or two is undecided*
- *That an experiment works on an existing implementation and a feature is a new one is recorded as
  an observation, stated by the engineer as a prediction rather than a rule*
- *Removing `code/ruby` from the default distribution … is recorded as an idea — Open rather than
  Active, because what a distribution is … are each undecided*

**The state-change entries are already right.** *The grouping is ext is realized* names the record
and the word it reached. Nothing about that form is in question.

## The rule is on the books, and the drift is from it

**The direction-and-sequencing-artifacts-take-a-log-entry rule says the entry records the artifact
rather than the word.** So the recent batch departed from a stated requirement rather than from
nothing. What the rule does not do is say what the departure looks like, and the departure is
grammatical rather than structural.

**The pull comes from the commit conventions, and they are right about commits.** The `git`
package's subject-first-commit-messages rule asks for the changed thing and its new state, in
passive voice and indicative mood. The commit-says-task-is-done-not-mark-done rule asks for the
fact rather than the clerical gesture. Both are written for a change to the software.

**An idea is not a change to the software.** The fact about it is that it was recorded, or that its
state moved. Writing its content in the same grammar states a fact that did not happen, which is
the one thing those rules never license.

## What it costs

**The log is the artifact a reader finds without holding anything else.** The
implementation-records-take-a-log-entry rule says so in its own words — the record answers *what was
tried* to someone already holding it, and the log answers *what has this project decided* to someone
holding nothing.

**That reader cannot tell proposed behavior from built behavior.** Every other Waytide record
carries a `**State:**` line. A log entry carries a title. Where the title asserts content, the
reader has nothing to weigh it against.

## The entries already written are not revised

**They are historical records.** The disclosure-is-the-one-reason-to-edit-a-historical-record rule
governs, and nothing here is a disclosure. This reaches what is written from now on.

**2026-08-24 — this no longer holds, and the engineer reversed it within the hour.** They directed
that the entries creating the wrong understanding be corrected. The section above keeps its wording,
per the amend-by-dated-addition practice, and the resolution below states what replaced it.

## Settled

- **2026-08-24 — an entry pertaining to an idea names the record and what happened to it.** That is
  the idea being recorded, or its `**State:**` line reaching a word. The idea's gist may follow, and
  it is attributed to the record rather than stated as the system's behavior.
- **2026-08-24 — an entry never states an unbuilt direction in the indicative mood.** This reaches
  the commit subject the entry travels in.
- **2026-08-24 — the six entries that assert unbuilt behavior are corrected**, on the engineer's
  instruction. Each title is rewritten to name the record and what happened to it, and each file is
  renamed to match, since a filename asserts the same claim in the listing the log is scanned by.
  The correction changes no fact — what each entry recorded is what it still records, in a mood that
  marks it as proposed.
- **2026-08-24 — the direction-and-sequencing-artifacts-take-a-log-entry rule carries it**, gaining
  a *What the entry says* section, a paragraph in its `**Why:**`, an instruction in its
  `**How to apply:**`, and the `git` grammar rules in its `Related:` list. Its title gains a clause,
  and its filename is unchanged. The alternative, a rule of its own, was put to the engineer and
  declined.
- **2026-08-24 — the record is Realized**, the rule that carries the obligation now existing. The
  three remaining Out of Scope items are excluded by decision rather than left undone.
- **2026-08-24 — the correction does not extend past those six.** The rest of the log is read and
  found sound: an entry reading indicatively elsewhere reports a rule that is in force, a feature
  that merged, or something that happened.

## Out of Scope / Deferred

- **Which rule carries it.** Amending the direction-and-sequencing-artifacts-take-a-log-entry rule
  is the candidate, since it already states the artifact-not-the-word requirement. A rule of its own
  is the alternative. This is undecided.
  - **2026-08-24 — decided, and this item leaves the tail.** The engineer selected the amendment,
    accepting the stated cost: the rule now says more than its name does, and its name is left
    alone rather than renamed, since a rename reaches every citation of it.
- **No wording template is fixed.** The earlier entries show a form that works. Fixing one would be
  the over-prescription the plans-no-code-samples rule warns against, and the form is not the part
  that failed.
- **Whether it reaches an implementation record's entries.** The engineer named the logs that
  pertain to an idea. An open feature or experiment raises the same question and is not settled
  here.
- **No conforming pass follows.** Recording this settles what is written from now on, per the
  `language` package's a-word-is-reserved-in-six-steps rule's statement that a conforming pass is
  asked for separately.
  - **2026-08-24 — the engineer asked for one separately, which is what that rule anticipates.** It
    was bounded to the six entries and is done. Nothing standing was conformed beyond them.
