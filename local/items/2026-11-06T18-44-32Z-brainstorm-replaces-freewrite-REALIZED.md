# The freewriting command might be `/brainstorm` rather than `/freewrite`

- **State:** Realized
- **Tags:** [idea]

**The command that records points as they are said, and organizes them at the end, would be typed `/brainstorm`, not `/freewrite`.** The item `2026-10-31T16-59-21Z` holds another candidate name for it, `/freeform`.

## Settled — 2026-11-06

**`/brainstorm` replaces `/freewrite`, and `/freewrite` is removed.**

**The `/freeform` alias is removed.**

**The activity is called brainstorming**, in the rule, its title, and the help. The rule is renamed to match.

**The work is done on `master`**, with no feature record.
