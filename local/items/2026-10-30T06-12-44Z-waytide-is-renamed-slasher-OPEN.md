# Waytide is renamed Slasher

- **State:** Open
- **Tags:** [idea]

**The system takes a new name: Slasher, in place of Waytide.** The name would reach everything that carries the present one: the packages and their repositories, the `waytide/` directory a consuming project installs into, the rules and records that name the system, the session-start notice and the startup display, and the README.

**Not yet settled:**

- why the name changes, and what *Slasher* names
- whether the `waytide/` directory in a consuming project is renamed, and how an installed project moves to the new name
- what becomes of the published component repositories and their history

## As first stated

In the engineer's words: "Idea: Rename Waytide to Slasher"
