# "Collect" might be "brainstorm"

- **State:** Realized
- **Tags:** [idea]

**The collecting commands might be named "brainstorm" in place of "collect".** The item `2026-10-29T02-42-44Z-a-command-protocol-begins-and-ends-a-recording` settles the commands as *begin collecting*, *end collecting*, and *resume collecting*.

**Not yet settled:**

- whether "brainstorm" replaces "collect"
- the words the three commands take with it, such as `/brainstorm begin`

**The two words name different acts.** *Collect* names the gathering of points that are said. *Brainstorm* names the producing of them. `COLLECT (v)` is approved in the STE dictionary. `brainstorm` is absent from it.

## Amended — 2026-10-29

**Two more candidates are named, "sketch" and "ideate", and the engineer prefers "sketch".** The engineer takes "sketch" as a synonym for "collect", or as an alias for it. An alias would leave the *collecting* commands as they are and add *sketch* as another wording of each. `sketch` and `ideate` are each absent from the STE dictionary.

**Not yet settled:** whether "sketch" is an alias beside "collect", or replaces it.

## Settled

- **2026-10-29** — The commands are named for "collect" and "sketch", each a wording of the same command. "Brainstorm" and "ideate" are not used.

## Amended — 2026-10-29: freewrite

**The word is "freewrite", and neither "collect" nor "sketch" is used.** This replaces the resolution above. "Brainstorm" names producing ideas, not capturing them unsorted to be organized later, and "braindump" and "spew" are colloquial.

## History

As the engineer first stated it: "Idea: “Collect” might be “brainstorm”"

On 2026-10-29: "It might also be “sketch” or “ideate”. I like “sketch”. I think that “collect” and “sketch” are synonyms or “sketch” is an alias for “collect”"

On 2026-10-29: "It will be collect and sketch"

On 2026-10-29, after "brainstorm", "sketch", "braindump", and "spew" were weighed: "Yup. “freewrite” is right". Offered whether it replaces only "sketch" or "collect" too, the engineer selected that it replaces both.
