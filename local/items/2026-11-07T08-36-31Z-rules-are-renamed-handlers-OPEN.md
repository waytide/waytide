# Rules are renamed handlers

- **State:** Open
- **Tags:** [idea]

**What Slasher calls a rule would be called a handler.** The word reaches far: the packages' `source/` rules, `rules-pre-loaded.md` and `rules-on-demand.md`, `local/rules/`, `load-rules.sh`, the *load rules* command, and the prose of nearly every rule, record, and vocabulary.

**Not yet settled:** which rules are meant, all of them or a subset; what a handler is said to handle; how far the rename reaches, from the prose alone to the file and directory names; and whether it is carried out as a migration.

## History

As the engineer first stated it: "rename rules to handlers"
