# A mistake is an item type

- **State:** Realized
- **Tags:** [idea]

**An item may be a mistake: something done wrong, recorded with what went wrong and why.** Where a problem is a difficulty that may have no author, a mistake is an act that should have gone otherwise, and recording it keeps what was learned from it.

**Not yet settled:**
- whether a mistake is a freeform tag or a reserved type with states of its own, such as one for a mistake that is corrected
- how a mistake differs from a problem
- what a mistake record carries, such as what happened, its cause, and its correction, and whether it names who made it
- how a mistake sits with the rule that a historical record is not revised, where the mistake is in one
