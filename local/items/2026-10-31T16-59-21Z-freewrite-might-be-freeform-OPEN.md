# "Freewrite" might be "freeform"

- **State:** Open
- **Tags:** [idea]

**The freewriting commands might be named "freeform" in place of "freewrite"**, as `/freeform begin`, `/freeform end`, and `/freeform resume`. The item `2026-10-29T19-18-01Z-collect-might-be-brainstorm` settles "freewrite", and the item `2026-10-29T16-24-42Z-waytide-skills` records the commands.

**The two words name different things.** *Freewrite* names an act, writing without stopping to order or judge. *Freeform* names a quality of what is produced, having no fixed form. "Freeform" is an adjective, so `/freeform begin` reads as a command named for the kind of text rather than for what the engineer does.

**Not yet settled:** whether "freeform" replaces "freewrite".
