# The participation question offers Unattended first

- **State:** Realized
- **Tags:** [idea]

**The question asked when a feature or an experiment starts, *Human in the loop*, would offer Unattended as its first option and Attended as its second.** Today Attended is first and Unattended second, as the attended-or-unattended-is-chosen-when-the-work-is-started rule fixes them.

**Not yet settled:** where Attended goes when the agent's confidence is 70% or lower and Attended is marked as the recommendation, since a first option reads as recommended.

## Settled

- **2026-11-07** — When the agent's confidence is 70% or lower and Attended is marked as the recommendation, Attended moves first, because a first option reads as recommended. In every other case, Unattended is first and Attended second. Selected by the engineer.
