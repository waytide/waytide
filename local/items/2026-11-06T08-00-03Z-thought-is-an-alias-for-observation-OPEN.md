# "Thought" is an alias for observation

- **State:** Open
- **Tags:** [idea]

**An observation could also be recorded as a "thought":** `/thought` would be an alias of `/item observation`, as `/observation` and `/observe` are, and `/item thought` would be an alias of `/item observation` within `/item`.

Both would record an observation, a working hypothesis whose one state, Recorded, never changes. Neither would make a new kind of item.

**Not yet settled:** whether `/thought promote` promotes an observation to a rule, as `/observation promote` does, and whether `/freewrite thought` follows.

As the engineer first stated it: "Iead: a "/thought" alias and "/item thought" alias for observation"
