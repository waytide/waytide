# The verdict question offers "What's a verdict"

- **State:** Open
- **Tags:** [idea]

**The question asked when a feature or an experiment starts, whether it concludes with or without an engineer's verdict, would also offer *What's a verdict*.** Selecting it would explain briefly what a verdict is: the engineer's declaration that the work is Affirmed, Refuted, or Inconclusive, against a forecast stated before the work. The question would then be asked again.

**Not yet settled:** whether the other start questions, the working location and *Human in the loop*, take a like option; and where the explanation's text comes from.

## History

As the engineer first stated it: "Also give the option of \"What's a verdict\""
