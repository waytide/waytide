# "Item" replaces "idea"

- **State:** Open
- **Tags:** [vocabulary]

**As the engineer stated it:** Instead of "idea", use "item".

## What the word names today

**An idea is a record in `waytide/local/ideas/`, and it is many kinds of record.** A design, a plan, an aspiration, an observation, and deferred work are each an idea, told apart by a tag. The ideas-convention fixes its states, its shape, and its log entry, and the an-idea-takes-a-log-entry rule is named for it.

**The word reaches far.** It names the directory, which holds 37 records, and it appears in 34 rule files, vocabularies, and scripts. The directory's name is in the session-start read instruction and in `session-start.sh`, which scans it for open work.
