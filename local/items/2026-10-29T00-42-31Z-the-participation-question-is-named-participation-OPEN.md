# The participation question is named "Participation" rather than "Who decides"

- **State:** Open
- **Tags:** [idea]

**The decision put at a feature's or an experiment's start is named "Participation" rather than "Who decides".** The design-by-efferent attended-or-unattended-is-chosen-at-the-start rule fixes the question's words as *Who decides?*, and the selection prompt carries them, in its question and in its header. The DBE vocabulary already names the decision **the human's level of participation**.

**Not yet settled:**

- whether "Participation" replaces the question's words, the prompt's header, or both
- how this sits with that rule's reason for its fixed words, that an engineer meeting the system can answer *Who decides* before meeting any reserved term

## Settled

- **2026-10-28** — "Participation" replaces "Who decides" in the prompt's question and in its header.
- **2026-10-28** — The prompt's question is "How will you take part?", and its header is "Participation". This replaces the question's words in the resolution above. The two options keep their words: "You decide. The agent automates." and "The agent decides and generates through."
- **2026-10-28** — The two options are labelled "Attended (human-in-the-loop)" and "Unattended (the agent makes the decisions)". Their descriptions keep the words in the resolution above.

## Amended — 2026-10-28

**The engineer judges "Who decides" unclear, and is not sure how the change sits with the rule's reason for fixed words.** A better wording than either may exist.

## History

As the engineer first stated it: "Idea: “Who decides” should be “Participation”"

On 2026-10-28, to the first open question: "Both". To the second: "I'm not sure, but “who decides” is unclear. Maybe there's a better option". Offered candidates for the question, the engineer selected "How will you take part?". Then: "For the “Attended” option, use “Attended (human-in-the-loop)”, and “Unattended (the agent makes the decisions)”".
