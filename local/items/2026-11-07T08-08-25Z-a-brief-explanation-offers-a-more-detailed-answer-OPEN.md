# A brief explanation offers a more detailed answer

- **State:** Open
- **Tags:** [idea]

**A brief explanation of a topic, as a bare `/slasher` gives one, would end with the option of a more detailed answer.** The engineer could then ask for the topic explained at more length without wording a new request.

**Not yet settled:** whether the option is a line of text, as the expansion line of the `language` package's an-answer-is-a-list-and-prose-must-justify-itself rule is, or a selection through the selection interface; and whether it reaches every brief explanation or only those a bare `/slasher` gives.

## History

As the engineer first stated it: "At the bottom of a breief description, give the option for a more detailed answer"
