# Loop record — Use ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English

The design of whether to adopt the AeroSpace and Defence Simplified Technical English
specification, run as an experiment on `experiment/simplified-technical-english`. Its questions,
forecast, and state are in the experiment record of the same name; this record carries the cycles.

**Mode: attended.** The loop stops at every hinge.

## Cycle 1 — what the experiment tests

- **Hinge:** which question the experiment asks. Three readings were available: the specification
  against the rule files, against the agent's answers, and against argumentative writing as such.
- **Options:** the three above, put through the selection interface.
- **Decision / chat:** the developer took the free-text escape and answered **all of the above**.
  The three are worked in an order the record fixes — what the specification costs argumentative
  writing first, since that answer informs both adoption questions rather than being independent
  of them.
- **What this costs, recorded now:** a single verdict over three questions is three answers wearing
  one word. The forecast predicts the experiment reaches **inconclusive** unless the question is
  narrowed before it concludes.

## Cycle 2 — how the work is conducted

- **Hinge:** the working location and the human's participation, settled at initiation and held for the
  experiment's duration.
- **Options:** branch only attended, branch and worktree attended, branch only unattended.
- **Decision / chat:** **branch only, attended.**

## Cycle 3 — what the experiment may conclude without the specification

- **Hinge:** the specification is not in this repository. What the agent holds is a general account
  of the approved dictionary and the writing rules, not the clauses.
- **Options:** none put. Stated as a constraint on the experiment rather than a choice, and
  recorded in the experiment's setup.
- **Decision / chat:** **no finding may rest on a specific rule number, a specific word's approval
  status, or an exact sentence-length limit.** The constraint is written down before the work so
  that a finding which quietly depends on one is visible as a defect rather than read as a result.
