# Loop record — Intuitive mode

The design of a rename of the human-centric project mode from **creative** to **intuitive**, and
the judgment of whether the new name is better. The experiment record is
`waytide/local/experiments/2026-08-05T18-14-20Z-intuitive-mode.md`, and it carries the forecast
committed before the work.

**Mode: unattended.** Chosen at initiation on 2026-08-05 beside the working location. The loop
does not gate: each cycle below records its hinge and the decision with the reasoning the agent
applied, and carries no Options or Decision / chat line, because neither happened.

## Cycle 1 — what the rename reaches, and what it deliberately does not

**Hinge.** The name appears in the published package, in this repository's own binding rule, in
live working artifacts, in historical records, and in five other repositories. Which of those the
rename crosses is the decision the whole experiment rests on, because it decides what is
reversible.

**Decided, and why.** The rename covers **`system/`** (ten files and the rule's own filename),
**this repository's local mode rule**, and the **live local artifacts** that cite the mode — a
deferred item and two observations. **Historical records are not touched**: nine log entries, the
project-mode feature record, and its loop record keep saying *creative*, because they state what
was decided when they were written.

**The five consuming projects are deliberately excluded.** This experiment is reversible because
it sits on a branch; changes to five other repositories are not covered by that reversibility, and
making them would convert an experiment into a migration before its question is answered. If the
experiment is affirmed, reaching those projects is separate work of the same shape as the
migration run the day before — each holds a binding local rule whose **filename carries the mode**.

## Cycle 2 — the local mode rule keeps its original timestamp

**Hinge.** This repository's mode rule is `2026-08-05T07-32-03Z-creative-mode.md`. Renaming it
raises whether the ISO-8601-UTC prefix moves with the name.

**Decided.** The **name part changes and the prefix does not** —
`2026-08-05T07-32-03Z-intuitive-mode.md`. The prefix records **when this project's mode was
decided**, which was 07:32:03Z and is not what changed. A new prefix would claim the project
adopted its mode at the moment the word was swapped, which is false, and would put the rule out
of order against the log entry that recorded the same decision.

## Cycle 3 — a defect a substitution cannot see

**Hinge.** How the substitution is performed across 46 occurrences.

**Decided, and what it cost.** A case-preserving replacement of *creative* with *intuitive*, which
was correct for every occurrence of the word itself and **broke the indefinite article in eight
places**: *a creative project* became *a intuitive project*. **The old word begins with a
consonant and the new one with a vowel**, so every `a` preceding it had to become `an` — a class
of error no search-and-replace can detect, because the defect is in the word before the one being
replaced. Corrected in a second pass, and recorded here because it is the kind of thing a rename
is assumed not to have.

## Outcome

The composite renamed; the five consuming projects untouched. `install.sh` parses. No occurrence
of the old name remains outside historical records and this experiment's own files, which
legitimately carry it as the name under test.
