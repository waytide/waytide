# I started simplifying Waytide

Mon Aug 17 2026

**Summary:** I had taken in too many of the biases I picked up from other agent meta-harnesses, so
I collapsed Waytide's record types into ideas, logs, and implementations, with tags carrying the
distinctions.

Yesterday, I started simplifying Waytide. I realized that I had taken in too many of the biases
I'd picked up from other agent meta-harnesses. I eliminated the planning and design directories,
the observations, and deferred items, and replaced them with "ideas". I consolidated all of the
records of work and action under a logs/ directory. Features and experiments are now also a single
record type under implementations/. Record categories are differentiated by applying tags. Tags are
now considered applicable to all record types.
