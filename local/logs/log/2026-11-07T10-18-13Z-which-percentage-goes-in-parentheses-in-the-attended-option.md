# Which percentage goes in parentheses in the Attended option, and how does it sit beside (Recommended)? — Attended estimate, merged, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> Which percentage goes in parentheses in the Attended option, and how does it sit beside (Recommended)?

The options:

- **Attended estimate, first** — The agent's estimate of its confidence if the work is attended, about the It'll work figure, ahead of (Recommended).
- **Attended estimate, merged** — The agent's estimate of its confidence if the work is attended, inside the same parentheses as Recommended.
- **Agent's confidence** — The same Agent's confidence figure the question already shows, the confidence of unattended work.

The engineer's answer: **Attended estimate, merged**
