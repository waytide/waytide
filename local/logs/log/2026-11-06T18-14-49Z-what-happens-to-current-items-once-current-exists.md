# What happens to /current-items once /current exists? — Remove it, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> What happens to /current-items once /current exists?

The options:

- **Remove it** — /current replaces /current-items. The old name stops working.
- **Keep as alias** — /current-items stays, as an alias of /current.

The engineer's answer: **Remove it**
