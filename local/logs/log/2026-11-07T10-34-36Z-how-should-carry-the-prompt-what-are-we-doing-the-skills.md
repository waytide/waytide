The question, put through the selection interface:

> How should /? carry the prompt "What are we doing?"? The skills rule says a skill's body stays thin: it loads the rule that governs the act and follows it.

The options:

- **A rule of its own** — A new on-demand foundation rule says that /? answers "What are we doing?", as if the engineer had typed it. The skill loads that rule.
- **In the skill only** — The skill itself says to answer "What are we doing?" as if the engineer had typed it, and loads no rule. This departs from the thin-skill rule.

The engineer's answer: **In the skill only**
