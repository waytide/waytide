# [STE] A vocabulary holds a fourth kind of entry — a word taken from the established vocabulary of structural design and software design, because the extant term already says the thing exactly. Waytide does not define these and uses them in their own sense, reserving them where the STE dictionary disputes them. `preserve` is the first, and the test that separates it from a term is whether the vocabulary says what the word means: `load-bearing` comes from structural engineering and design-by-efferent defines it, so it is a term, while `preserve` is defined nowhere.
