# `reserve` stands over `claim` as Waytide's word for stating that a word is a technical noun or a technical verb in a category, and the ground is STE Rule 1.13 rather than incumbency — `claim` names the act more accurately, since what happens is an assertion a reader can check and Rule 1.8 asks the company to issue, but Rule 1.13 forbids a technical verb used as a noun and `claim` has no separate noun form, so *a claim* would be barred wherever it appears, while `reserve` has `reservation`; the corpus also needs `claim` as an ordinary noun, as in a claim about other people's code; both words are absent from the STE dictionary, so whichever won was itself a reservation in the ETE deliberation-and-speech category
