# What does the summary show for each current implementation? — Title and state, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> What does the summary show for each current implementation?

The options:

- **Title and state** — One row each: handle, title (Feature — … or Experiment — …), and state.
- **Add branch and phase** — As title and state, plus its branch and, where it runs in phases, its current phase.
- **Add the intent** — As title and state, plus its question or intent in one sentence.

The engineer's answer: **Title and state**
