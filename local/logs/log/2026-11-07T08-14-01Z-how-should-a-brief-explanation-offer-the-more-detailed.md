# How should a brief explanation offer the more detailed answer? — A line of text, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> How should a brief explanation offer the more detailed answer?

The options:

- **A line of text** — The explanation ends with a line such as: For a more detailed answer, ask for one, or type /expand. Nothing waits for a reply.
- **A selection** — The explanation ends with a question through the selection interface: More detail, or Done. Selecting More detail gives the longer answer.
- **A selection of depth** — The question offers how much more: a fuller explanation, or a worked example from an ordinary project, or Done.

The engineer's answer: **A line of text**
