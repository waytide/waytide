# The language rule will say a brief explanation always ends with the expansion line. What counts as a brief explanation? — Any compressed answer, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> The language rule will say a brief explanation always ends with the expansion line. What counts as a brief explanation?

The options:

- **Asked for as brief** — An explanation asked for as brief: a topic chosen at a bare /slasher, or a request worded "Briefly explain…".
- **Any explanation** — Any explanation the engineer asked for, brief or not, since every answer is compressed by default.
- **Any compressed answer** — Any answer the agent compressed, explanation or not. The line stops being a judgment and appears on every such answer.

The engineer's answer: **Any compressed answer**
