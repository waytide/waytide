# The vocabulary preambles carry two statuses rather than three, and the entry names the category — a word is approved in the STE dictionary and used with its approved meaning, or it is reserved as a technical noun or a technical verb in a category the entry names, which is an STE category under STE Rules 1.5, 1.6, or 1.12, or an ETE category under the ete-declares-a-noun-category-and-a-verb-category rule; the third status, an approved word used with a meaning Waytide does not use, is now a reservation in an ETE category governed by the ete-fixes-the-meaning-of-a-word-in-its-category rule; the blanket claim of category 19 for every term in a file is gone, since a category belongs to a word and not to a file; and `reserve` stands as the one word for the act, which STE Rule 1.11 requires, so the two new rules give up `claim` for it
