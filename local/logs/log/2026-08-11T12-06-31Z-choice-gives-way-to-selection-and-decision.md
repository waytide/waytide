# "choice" gives way to "selection" and "decision", the two approved words that already carried the two senses — `choice (n)` is not approved in the ASD-STE100 dictionary and its stated alternative is SELECTION (n), whose approved meaning is the action or result of choosing; the corpus already used `selection` for the UI and `decision` for the matter settled, so Rule 1.11 forbade carrying a third word for the same items; eighty uses were replaced one at a time, `selection` where the engineer picks at a prompt and `decision` where the sentence means the matter being settled, and the substitution is recorded in the language vocabulary with its STE note
