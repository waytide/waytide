# Controls: what does the pretend old project hold before the installer runs? — Record and packages, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> Controls: what does the pretend old project hold before the installer runs?

The options:

- **One record** — Only waytide/local/items/ with one record in it. Nothing else the check doesn't read.
- **Record and packages** — The record, plus waytide/system/foundation/ holding the real plugin folder, as an installed project has.

The engineer's answer: **Record and packages**
