# Does /unattended show a plan before it works? — Plan first, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> Does /unattended show a plan before it works?

The options:

- **No plan** — It starts at once. The engineer reads the result.
- **Plan first** — It shows a plan through the selection interface, as unattended work in a feature does, and waits for Accept.

The engineer's answer: **Plan first**
