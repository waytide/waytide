# What is the test named where an old project's waytide/ ends up as slasher/, with waytide/ gone? — Moves waytide/ to slasher/, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> What is the test named where an old project's waytide/ ends up as slasher/, with waytide/ gone?

The options:

- **Moves waytide/ to slasher/** — Names the act by the two folders.
- **Renames waytide/ to slasher/** — Names it a rename, since the folder keeps its place and changes its name.
- **Moves the project's work to slasher/** — Names what the engineer cares about, their records, rather than the folder.

The engineer's answer: **Moves waytide/ to slasher/**
