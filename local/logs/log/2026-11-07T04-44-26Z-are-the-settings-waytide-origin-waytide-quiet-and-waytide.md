# Are the settings WAYTIDE_ORIGIN, WAYTIDE_QUIET, and WAYTIDE_REGISTRY renamed? — SLASHER_ only, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> Are the settings WAYTIDE_ORIGIN, WAYTIDE_QUIET, and WAYTIDE_REGISTRY renamed?

The options:

- **SLASHER_, old still read** — They become SLASHER_ORIGIN and so on. The scripts still read a WAYTIDE_ setting where the SLASHER_ one isn't set.
- **SLASHER_ only** — They become SLASHER_ORIGIN and so on. A WAYTIDE_ setting is ignored.
- **Keep WAYTIDE_** — The settings keep their names.

The engineer's answer: **SLASHER_ only**
