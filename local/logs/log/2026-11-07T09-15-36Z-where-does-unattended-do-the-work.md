# Where does /unattended do the work? — Request decides, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> Where does /unattended do the work?

The options:

- **Where it is** — On the branch the working tree is on, creating nothing, as "Do #10 on your own" did.
- **As a feature** — It starts a feature, unattended, and the agent picks the working location.
- **Request decides** — Where the request names a feature or an item to start, as a feature; otherwise where it is.

The engineer's answer: **Request decides**
