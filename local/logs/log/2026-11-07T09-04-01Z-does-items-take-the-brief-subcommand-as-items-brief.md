# Does /items take the brief subcommand, as /items brief? — "/brief" becomes it's own command, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> Does /items take the brief subcommand, as /items brief?

The options:

- **No brief** — /items lists only. The brief report stays /report brief and /brief.
- **Takes brief** — /items brief says what was recently done and what's next, as /report brief does.

The engineer's answer: **"/brief" becomes it's own command**
