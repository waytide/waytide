# The an-entry-is-the-writers-words rule gains the case it had no words for — the writer directing a change to their own entry, which is the record's author acting on the record rather than the agent deciding an entry should read differently; the agent makes exactly the change named, leaves everything else as written, and logs it, and the entry keeps its Authored by-only footer so the log carries the trace, which is the shape the disclosure rule already uses
