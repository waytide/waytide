# Promoting an observation to a rule is suspended, with its command, its Promoted to line, and its log entry

**Tags:** [decision] [suspended]

The promotion tied a rule to the observation it came from, and bought a link the rule's own text and the log already give. Removing it drops the `promote` subcommand from `/observation` and `/observe`, its line from the item help, and the promotion wording from the observation rule, the ask-at-once rule, and the items rule's Related list. A rule is still written from settled thinking, the way any rule is written, and the observation stays as written.
